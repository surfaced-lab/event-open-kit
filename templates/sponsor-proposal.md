# Sponsor proposal skeleton

> This is a public structure, not a real offer. Use verified audience and media evidence only. Keep negotiated pricing, private contacts, and customer information outside this repository.

## Public summary

`[Organization]` can support `[event]` by funding `[specific public outcome]` for `[community audience]`.

## Fit

- Community need: `[source-backed need]`
- Sponsor objective: `[brand or community objective]`
- Participant benefit: `[concrete benefit]`
- Public proof route: `[canonical page or report]`

## Package structure

| Layer | Public deliverable | Evidence owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presence | `[event-page placement]` | `[role]` |
| Utility | `[participant-facing benefit]` | `[role]` |
| Recognition | `[thank-you or recap artifact]` | `[role]` |
| Review | `[post-event evidence summary]` | `[role]` |

## Guardrails

- No claim of reach, registrations, revenue, or renewal without a named measurement source.
- No use of personal data in public examples.
- No automatic outreach from this template.
- Final price, rights, approval, and delivery terms belong in a private agreement.
